========================================================
E-COMMERCE RETURN, REFUND & WARRANTY POLICY (2026 EDITION)
========================================================

1. Return Window:
Customers can request a full refund for any standard item purchased on our platform within 30 calendar days of delivery. 

2. Eligibility Requirements:
To be eligible for a full refund, the product must be unused, in its original packaging, and accompanied by the original receipt or order confirmation. 

3. Extended Store Credit Window:
Items returned after 30 days but within 60 days of delivery will receive store credit instead of a cash refund. Returns after 60 days are not accepted.

4. Electronics & Special Category Items:
Laptops, smartphones, audio equipment, and custom-manufactured products have a strict 14-day return window from the date of delivery.

5. Shipping Costs:
Return shipping fees are the responsibility of the customer, unless the delivered product was damaged, defective, or incorrect upon arrival. In such cases, we provide a prepaid shipping label.

6. Refund Processing Time:
Once the returned package is received and inspected at our central warehouse, refund processing takes 3 to 5 business days, and funds are credited back to the original payment method.
